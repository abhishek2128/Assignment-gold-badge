HCL is an Indian multinational information technology (IT) services and consulting company headquartered in Noida, India. Founded in 1976 by Shiv Nadar, HCL initially began as a hardware company focusing on microcomputers and early computing systems. Over time, it strategically transformed itself into a global IT services organization offering software development, infrastructure management, engineering services, and digital transformation solutions.

Today, HCL operates in more than 50 countries across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East. The company employs over 225,000 professionals worldwide, making it one of the largest IT service providers in India. HCL is known for its employee-first philosophy, which focuses on empowering employees through innovation, skill development, and leadership opportunities.

HCL offers a wide range of services including IT and business services, engineering and R&D services, cloud computing, cybersecurity solutions, artificial intelligence integration, data analytics, and enterprise application development. Its major business divisions include HCL Technologies, HCL Infosystems, and HCL Software.

The company follows a global delivery model, combining onsite and offshore development to provide cost-effective and scalable solutions for clients. HCL has long-standing partnerships with several Fortune 500 companies, helping them modernize legacy systems and adopt next-generation technologies.

Innovation is a core pillar of HCL’s strategy. The company invests heavily in research and development centers and collaborates with global technology leaders. Its “Mode 1-2-3 strategy” focuses on core services, new growth areas, and future digital technologies.

HCL is also committed to sustainability and corporate social responsibility (CSR). It runs multiple initiatives in education, healthcare, skill development, rural empowerment, and environmental sustainability. Through its CSR programs, HCL aims to bridge the digital divide and promote inclusive growth.

Additionally, HCL emphasizes continuous learning and employee development through structured training programs, leadership academies, and certification platforms. It encourages a culture of experimentation, creativity, and collaboration across teams.

Overall, HCL stands as one of India’s leading global IT companies, known for its strong engineering heritage, innovation-driven culture, and commitment to sustainable growth.
