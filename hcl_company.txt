HCL is an Indian multinational IT company headquartered in Noida, India. Founded in 1976, it started as a hardware company and gradually expanded into software services, IT consulting, and business solutions globally.

The company operates in over 50 countries and has a diverse workforce of more than 200,000 employees. HCL is recognized for its strong focus on innovation, research, and collaboration across various industries, including finance, healthcare, and manufacturing.

HCL provides IT services, engineering, digital transformation, cloud computing, and cybersecurity solutions. Its business model emphasizes customer-centricity, and it partners with several Fortune 500 companies to deliver tailored technology solutions.

In addition to technology services, HCL invests heavily in employee training and leadership development programs. The company encourages a culture of continuous learning and supports various initiatives to foster creativity and innovation among its workforce.

HCL is committed to corporate social responsibility (CSR) and sustainability. It runs programs in education, health, and community development and actively participates in global initiatives to reduce carbon footprint and promote digital inclusion.
